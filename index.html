About Me

My professional foundation is built on logistics and operational excellence. I hold a Bachelor of Logistics and Supply Chain Management with First Class Honours from Dhurakij Pundit University.

Driven by curiosity, I taught myself HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and SQL. These skills allowed me to build practical tools like ShopThip and Moo Pak Zap, turning conceptual ideas into functional digital experiences.
